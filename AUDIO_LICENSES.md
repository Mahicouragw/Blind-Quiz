The application does not use Web Audio oscillators, generated beeps, AI-generated audio, or placeholder recordings. Essential questions, choices, feedback, timers, and scores are available as text. Screen-reader live-region announcements use the user’s own assistive technology and are not bundled media assets.

Do not add an audio file until its exact recording has been listened to, matched to the intended clue, and documented here with asset name, source URL, creator, license terms permitting in-game use and redistribution, required attribution, date checked, and intended use. Mixkit music must not be used in this game.
