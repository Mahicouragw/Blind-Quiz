                          PRIVATE · END-TO-END ENCRYPTED

                          Chat

                          Only you and your friend can read these messages. They are locked on your device before they are sent, and the game server cannot read or change them. Each device has its own key, so older messages stay readable only on the device that received them.

                            Calls and files

                            Audio and video calls, voice messages, and files work while you are both online. They go directly between your devices and are not kept in chat history. Files can be up to 2 GB; the temporary copy in Blind Quiz is removed after three hours. If you save or share a copy elsewhere, that copy is yours to manage.

                              Room chat

                                Room chat and voice messages are heard by everyone in the room. Voice messages stop at 60 seconds; preview and press Send, or discard them. Playback styles and speeds are free, local choices; the original recording stays unchanged. Messages are deleted after 24 hours. For private messages, use Message on a friend.
